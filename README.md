## Overview

One popular and effective method to learn a new language is using comprehensible input. In this approach,
you watch videos in your target language such that the video is mostly comprehensible.
Overtime, you will gradually and naturally acquire vocabulary and grammar from the target language.
As you progress, you will have to increase the difficulty of the videos to continue learning.

Selecting the appropriate videos to watch is not an easy task. This is why even with the wealth of content
available to us (via YouTube, Netflex, etc.), we have dedicated websites to help curate these videos for us.

The goal of this repo is to build an extensive and ever-growing catalog of comprehensible content ranked
by difficulty. The videos will be curated from YouTube and are currently limited to Spanish content.

## Difficulty Score

The difficulty score soley relies on linguistic features (like speed of speech, unique vocabulary, etc.) and tuned via
a linear regression model. As such, this score is independent of channels and allows us to compare any video.
The difficulty level will be granular enough for anyone to progress seemlessly as they acquire more of the language.

## Current and Future Scope

Currently, only a couple channels are provided, but more will be coming.
